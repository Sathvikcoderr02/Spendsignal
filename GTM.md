# GTM (Go-To-Market)

The exact target user is a **Founder or Engineering Manager at a 5–40 person startup** that already uses 2 or more AI tools (for example Cursor + ChatGPT + one API). This user feels AI spend pain but has no time to manually compare plans every month.

Right before they need this tool, they usually:

- search “how to reduce ChatGPT team cost”
- search “Cursor vs Copilot pricing”
- read posts about “AI tool stack for startups”
- complain in Slack/X about rising AI bill

Where they hang out online:

- Subreddits: `r/startups`, `r/SaaS`, `r/Entrepreneur`, `r/SideProject`
- Communities: Indie Hackers, YC Startup School forum, Product Hunt makers
- Slack/Discord: founder circles, small dev agency groups, build-in-public groups
- X: founders who post monthly burn updates, AI builders, indie hackers

## First 100 users in 30 days with $0 paid budget

1. Launch a useful “free AI spend audit” thread on X with 3 real examples.
2. Post in Indie Hackers with a before/after sample report and ask for feedback.
3. Share in 5 founder/developer Slack groups with a no-login angle.
4. Publish a short “AI spend mistakes” post on LinkedIn and Medium.
5. Ask first 15 users to share their public audit link (viral loop).
6. DM founders who recently posted about AI costs and offer free audit.

Target activity volume:

- 2 strong posts/week
- 5 direct outreach DMs/day
- 3 community posts/week
- 1 mini case-study/week

## The unfair channel

Credex already has the strongest unfair channel: **discounted AI infrastructure credits** plus direct context about where startup AI money gets wasted. Most audit tools can only point to a problem. Credex can both show savings and provide a path to capture those savings.

## Week-1 traction if this works

Good week-1 signs:

- 300 landing visitors
- 120 completed audits (40% completion)
- 25 email captures (21% of completed audits)
- 8 high-savings leads (`>$500/mo`)
- 3 consultation bookings

If this happens, the loop is working: users get value first, then some of them convert to deeper help.
