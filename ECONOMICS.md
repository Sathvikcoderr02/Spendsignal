# Economics

## 1) What is a converted lead worth?

I use rough but practical numbers.

Assume one converted customer buys discounted credits through Credex with average monthly gross profit of **$600**.  
If average retention is **10 months**, lifetime gross profit is:

`$600 x 10 = $6,000`

So one converted lead is worth about **$6,000 gross profit LTV**.

## 2) CAC by channel from GTM plan

Since this is mostly organic, cash spend is near zero, but time cost is real.

Assume founder time cost = **$50/hour**.

- X + community posting: 20 hours/month = $1,000
- Direct outreach + replies: 15 hours/month = $750
- Writing case studies: 10 hours/month = $500

Total monthly effort cost: **$2,250**

If this brings 40 qualified leads/month:

`CAC per qualified lead = $2,250 / 40 = $56.25`

If 6 of those leads become paying credit buyers:

`CAC per customer = $2,250 / 6 = $375`

## 3) What conversion rates make it profitable?

Funnel example:

- 1,000 audit completions/month
- 12% book consultation = 120
- 20% of consults purchase credits = 24 customers

Revenue quality check:

- CAC per customer around $375 (from above)
- LTV gross profit around $6,000
- LTV/CAC = `6000 / 375 = 16x`

Even with much worse rates (say 6% consult booking and 12% close), this can still be profitable because acquisition cost is low.

## 4) What must be true for $1M ARR in 18 months?

If average net monthly revenue per active customer is **$350**, annual is:

`$350 x 12 = $4,200 per customer per year`

To reach $1,000,000 ARR:

`$1,000,000 / $4,200 = ~239 active customers`

So by month 18, we need about **240 active paying customers**.

One possible path:

- Month 1–6: prove funnel, reach 35 customers
- Month 7–12: improve conversion + referrals, reach 110 total
- Month 13–18: stronger partnerships + repeatable outbound, reach 240 total

## 5) Bottom line

This tool works economically if:

1. Audit completion volume grows steadily.
2. At least 8–12% of audits turn into consult calls.
3. At least 15–20% of consults convert to purchases.
4. Organic channels keep CAC low.

The most important lever is not just more traffic. It is **high trust in savings math**, because trust drives consultation bookings.
