## FAQ (5 real Q&As)

### 1) Do I need to sign up before seeing results?
No. You can run the full audit first. Email is optional and shown after value.

### 2) Is this using real pricing data?
Yes. Pricing is pulled from official vendor pricing pages and listed in `PRICING_DATA.md`.

### 3) How are savings calculated?
Savings come from rule-based checks: right-size plans, lower-cost alternatives by use case, and credit discount scenarios where valid.

### 4) What happens with my data?
Public share links remove private details. Email and company fields are stored only if you submit lead capture.

### 5) What if my stack is already optimized?
The audit says that clearly. We do not force fake savings. You can still opt in for future optimization alerts.
